# AI-Whisperers Content Quality Checklist

**Document Type:** Pre-Publish Review Criteria
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2025-10-20
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This checklist ensures every LinkedIn post meets AI-Whisperers brand standards before publication. Use this at **Stage 2 (Quality Check)** of the publishing workflow.

**Rule:** If any item fails, revise the post before proceeding to founder review.

---

## Content Standards

### Metrics & Claims
- [ ] **Specific metrics cited** (30%, 18%, 2 weeks, etc.) OR clearly labeled "anecdotal"
- [ ] **Metrics are traceable** to real pilots/projects (30% triage, 18% hotfixes)
- [ ] **No exaggeration** ("reduced" not "eliminated," "improved by X%" not "completely transformed")
- [ ] **Timeframes included** when relevant (6 weeks, 2-week pilot, 20 minutes)

### Tone & Language
- [ ] **No buzzwords** ("AI magic," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "disruptive")
- [ ] **Pragmatic tone** (builder-focused, not hype-driven)
- [ ] **Clear language** (simple sentences, no jargon unless necessary)
- [ ] **First person if founder voice** ("I built..." not "We offer...") OR third person if company voice

### Value & Utility
- [ ] **Example is concrete** (not vague: "We built a classifier with 4 lanes" not "We used AI to help")
- [ ] **Takeaway is actionable** (reader can apply the lesson: "Start with one workflow...")
- [ ] **Focus on "how" and "why"** (not just "what")
- [ ] **Clarity over cleverness** (straightforward > overly creative)

## Structure Checklist

### Hook (1-2 sentences)
- [ ] **Grabs attention** (metric, question, or contrarian statement)
- [ ] **Sets up the post** (reader knows what to expect)
- [ ] **Concise** (doesn't bury the lead)

**Good Example:** "We cut ticket handle time by 30% with an AI triage agent. Here's how we did it in 2 weeks."

**Bad Example:** "Have you ever wondered if AI could help your team be more productive? We think it can!"

---

### Insight (2-3 sentences)
- [ ] **Explains "why"** (context, problem, or lesson learned)
- [ ] **Builds credibility** (shows expertise or experience)
- [ ] **Bridges hook to example** (logical flow)

**Good Example:** "Most support queues fail because everything looks urgent. The triage agent doesn't solve tickets — it sorts them so humans focus on what matters."

**Bad Example:** "We've been working on automation for a while and think it's really important for companies today."

---

### Example (2-4 sentences)
- [ ] **Includes numbers** (metrics, timeframes, scale)
- [ ] **Specific details** (4 lanes, 2,000 tickets, 20 minutes)
- [ ] **Describes outcome** (what changed: 30% faster resolution)
- [ ] **Relatable to audience** (target audience can see themselves in the scenario)

**Good Example:** "We built a simple classifier that routes tickets into 4 lanes: auto-resolve, low-touch, complex, and escalate. Trained on 2,000 historical tickets, deployed in 20 minutes. Result: 30% faster resolution, happier agents."

**Bad Example:** "We used our proprietary AI technology to optimize the workflow and saw great results."

---

### Takeaway (1-2 sentences)
- [ ] **Actionable principle** (reader can apply it)
- [ ] **Clear and memorable** (could be quoted/shared)
- [ ] **Reinforces core message** (ties back to hook and insight)

**Good Example:** "Start with one workflow. Scope it tight. Measure one metric. Repeat."

**Bad Example:** "Automation is powerful and everyone should use it."

---

### CTA (Call-to-Action, 1 sentence)
- [ ] **Invites engagement** (question, invitation, offer)
- [ ] **Natural, not pushy** (conversational tone)
- [ ] **Relevant to post** (connects to the topic)

**Good Examples:**
- "What's one support workflow you'd automate first?"
- "Follow for more frameworks on building multi-agent systems."
- "DM me if you want the full pilot playbook."

**Bad Examples:**
- "Like and share if you agree!"
- "Visit our website to learn more." (LinkedIn deprioritizes links in posts)

## Formatting Checklist

### Word Count
- [ ] **120-180 words total** (use word counter)
- [ ] **If under 120 words:** Add detail to example or takeaway
- [ ] **If over 180 words:** Cut fluff, tighten sentences

---

### Emojis
- [ ] **Maximum 2 emojis** (preferably 0-1)
- [ ] **Placement:** Hook or Example section (not scattered throughout)
- [ ] **Avoid:** Emoji-only bullets, excessive decoration, unprofessional emojis

**Good:** "30% faster resolution ⚡"
**Bad:** "🚀🔥💯 AI is amazing!!! 🎉🎊"

---

### Hashtags
- [ ] **Exactly 4 hashtags** (no more, no less)
- [ ] **2 Broad tags** (e.g., #Automation, #AI, #DevOps, #Productivity)
- [ ] **2 Niche tags** (e.g., #MultiAgentSystems, #RepoHealth, #AIWhisperers)
- [ ] **No spaces in hashtags** (#MultiAgentSystems not #Multi Agent Systems)
- [ ] **Placed at end of post** (not mid-text)

**Hashtag Master List:** See `outputs/CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` for approved tags

---

### Line Breaks & Spacing
- [ ] **Line breaks for readability** (1-2 blank lines between sections)
- [ ] **Short paragraphs** (2-3 sentences max per paragraph)
- [ ] **No walls of text** (avoid dense blocks)

---

### Grammar & Proofreading
- [ ] **No typos** (proofread twice)
- [ ] **Correct punctuation** (especially quotes, apostrophes)
- [ ] **Consistent tense** (don't switch between past/present unnecessarily)
- [ ] **Run through Grammarly or Hemingway** (optional but recommended)

---

## Brand Alignment Checklist

### Tone of Voice (See BRAND_BRIEF.md)
- [ ] **Professional yet approachable** (expert without being condescending)
- [ ] **Clear** (simple language, no jargon unless necessary)
- [ ] **Builder-minded** ("We built..." "I helped teams...")
- [ ] **Pragmatic** (evidence over hype, results over promises)

**Voice Test:** Would Kyrian (founder) say this in a 1:1 conversation with a DevOps lead or operations manager?

---

### Values Alignment
- [ ] **Measurable** (every claim backed by data or labeled "anecdotal")
- [ ] **Transparent** (honest about mistakes, clear about process)
- [ ] **Human-centered** (AI assists people, doesn't replace judgment)
- [ ] **Iterative** (emphasis on starting small, measuring early)

---

### Target Audience Relevance
- [ ] **Speaks to operations managers, DevOps leads, or founders** (primary audiences)
- [ ] **Addresses a real pain point** (invisible debt, ticket overload, tech sprawl)
- [ ] **Offers practical value** (framework, lesson, metric to track)

**Audience Test:** Would a DevOps lead or operations manager find this useful enough to save or share?

---

## Red Flags (Automatic Fail)

If ANY of these are present, revise immediately:

- ❌ **Buzzwords:** "AI magic," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "disruptive," "synergy"
- ❌ **Vague claims:** "dramatically improved," "significantly better" (without numbers)
- ❌ **Overpromising:** "AI will solve all your problems," "completely eliminate..."
- ❌ **No proof:** Metrics cited but not traceable to real projects
- ❌ **Salesy tone:** "Our cutting-edge solution," "Contact us today!"
- ❌ **Unprofessional:** Excessive emojis, all caps, clickbait
- ❌ **Off-brand:** Hype-driven, overly technical jargon, academic language

---

## Green Flags (Quality Indicators)

If MOST of these are present, post is likely strong:

- ✅ **Specific metrics** (30%, 18%, 2 weeks, 20 minutes)
- ✅ **Concrete example** (4 lanes, 2,000 tickets, triage agent)
- ✅ **Actionable takeaway** ("Start with one workflow...")
- ✅ **Honest tone** (shares mistakes: "3 things we got wrong...")
- ✅ **Clear value** (reader learns a framework, lesson, or metric)
- ✅ **Conversational CTA** (question, not demand)
- ✅ **Brand consistency** (matches BRAND_BRIEF.md tone)

---

## Quick Reference: The 5-Second Test

Read the post aloud. Ask:

1. **Does it sound like a builder talking to a builder?** (Not a salesperson)
2. **Is there a specific, traceable metric?** (30%, 18%, or labeled "anecdotal")
3. **Can the reader apply the lesson?** (Actionable takeaway)
4. **Is it free of buzzwords?** (No "AI magic," "revolutionary")
5. **Would Kyrian approve this?** (Brand voice alignment)

**If YES to all 5 → Post is likely ready for review**
**If NO to any → Revise before submitting**

## Example: Checklist in Action

### Sample Post (Before Checklist)
```
We used AI to improve our support process and saw amazing results! Our revolutionary triage system is a game-changer. Contact us to learn more about how we can transform your operations! 🚀🔥💯

#AI #Innovation #Tech #Business
```

### Issues Identified
- ❌ Buzzwords: "amazing," "revolutionary," "game-changer," "transform"
- ❌ No specific metrics
- ❌ Salesy tone ("Contact us...")
- ❌ Excessive emojis (3)
- ❌ Vague ("improve," "saw results")
- ❌ Wrong hashtags (no niche tags)

---

### Sample Post (After Checklist)
```
We cut ticket handle time by 30% with an AI triage agent. Here's how we did it in 2 weeks.

Most support queues fail because everything looks urgent. The triage agent doesn't solve tickets — it sorts them so humans focus on what matters.

We built a simple classifier that routes tickets into 4 lanes: auto-resolve, low-touch, complex, and escalate. Trained on 2,000 historical tickets, deployed in 20 minutes ⚡

Result: 30% faster resolution, happier agents, fewer burnouts.

The key: we didn't try to automate everything. We automated triage, then let humans do what they do best — solve complex problems.

Start with one workflow. Scope it tight. Measure one metric. Repeat.

What's one support workflow you'd automate first?

#Automation #Operations #MultiAgentSystems #AI
```

### Checklist Result: ✅ PASS
- ✅ Specific metrics (30%, 2 weeks, 20 minutes)
- ✅ Concrete example (4 lanes, 2,000 tickets)
- ✅ Actionable takeaway ("Start with one workflow...")
- ✅ No buzzwords
- ✅ 1 emoji (within limit)
- ✅ 4 hashtags (2 broad + 2 niche)
- ✅ Builder tone, conversational CTA
