## Mission & Positioning

### Mission Statement
Bring clarity, transparency, and measurable outcomes to AI automation by building reliable multi-agent systems that replace repetitive work in operations and software teams.

### Core Idea
Small, scoped AI agents replacing repetitive work — no hype, just practical results.

### Positioning
AI-Whisperers designs "agentized" workflows that turn standard operating procedures (SOPs) into measurable automation. We focus on ticket triage, repo health monitoring, and service operations that deliver concrete ROI in weeks, not quarters.

## Tone of Voice

### Core Tone Attributes
- **Professional yet approachable** - Expert without being condescending
- **Clear** - Simple language, no jargon unless necessary
- **Builder-minded** - We're makers talking to makers
- **Pragmatic** - Evidence over hype, results over promises

### Do's
- Use first person when speaking as founder ("I built...")
- Share lessons learned, including mistakes
- Cite specific numbers and timeframes
- Focus on "how" and "why," not just "what"
- Use technical terms accurately (e.g., "multi-agent," "autonomous")

### Don'ts
- **Avoid buzzwords:** "AI magic," "revolutionary," "disruptive," "game-changing"
- **Don't exaggerate:** "Completely eliminated" → "Reduced by X%"
- **Don't oversimplify:** "AI does it all" → "AI triages, humans decide"
- **Don't be vague:** "Improved efficiency" → "Cut handle time by 30%"

### Bilingual Approach
- **Primary language:** English (for maximum reach)
- **Spanish usage:** Light phrases acceptable when adding authenticity
- **Technical terms:** Keep in English (e.g., "multi-agent systems," "repo health")
- **Comments/DMs:** Match language of commenter/recipient

## Content Standards (Quality Rules)

### Non-Negotiables (from LINKEDINCONTEXT.md Phase 8)
1. **No buzzwords or vague "AI magic"** - Be specific and technical
2. **Every metric must be traceable or clearly labeled as anecdotal**
3. **Focus on clarity, utility, and credibility** - Value over flash
4. **Empathy required** - "Builders talking to builders"

### Post Structure
- **Hook** - Open with a question, metric, or contrarian statement
- **Insight** - Share the "why" or lesson learned
- **Example** - Concrete use case with numbers
- **Takeaway** - Actionable principle or framework
- **CTA** - Invite conversation or offer resource

### Formatting Guidelines
- **Length:** 120-180 words per post
- **Emojis:** Maximum 2 per post
- **Hashtags:** 2 broad (#Automation, #AI, #Productivity, #DevOps) + 2 niche (#MultiAgentSystems, #ParaguayTech, #AIWhisperers)
- **Line breaks:** Use spacing for readability
- **Links:** Always provide context before link

## Quick Reference Card

**When in doubt, ask:**
1. Is this claim backed by a specific metric or labeled "anecdotal"?
2. Would a DevOps lead or operations manager find this useful?
3. Is the tone pragmatic and builder-focused (not hype-driven)?
4. Does it show our work (process + lessons), not just results?
5. Would Kyrian (founder) say this in a 1:1 conversation?

**Red flags:**
- Words like "revolutionary," "game-changing," "AI magic"
- Vague claims ("dramatically improved" vs. "reduced by 30%")
- No clear CTA or takeaway
- More than 2 emojis or overly casual tone
- Missing proof points or examples
