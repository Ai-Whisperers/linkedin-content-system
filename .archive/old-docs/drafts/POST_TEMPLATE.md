## Post Structure (120-180 words)

### Hook (1-2 sentences)
**Purpose:** Grab attention with a question, metric, or contrarian statement

[Write your hook here]

**Examples:**
- "30% faster ticket resolution. Here's how we did it."
- "Why is your AI strategy failing? Probably missing this one thing."
- "We reduced hotfixes by 18% in 6 weeks. No magic, just measurement."

---

### Insight (2-3 sentences)
**Purpose:** Share the "why" or lesson learned

[Write your insight here]

**Examples:**
- "Most teams try to automate before documenting. That's backwards. SOPs are the foundation, AI is the accelerator."
- "The triage agent didn't replace our support team. It freed them to focus on complex issues that need human judgment."

---

### Example (2-4 sentences)
**Purpose:** Concrete use case with numbers or specific details

[Write your example here]

**Guidelines:**
- Use specific metrics (30%, 18%, 2 weeks, etc.)
- Name the tool/agent/workflow
- Describe what changed (before → after)
- Label anecdotal data if not validated

---

### Takeaway (1-2 sentences)
**Purpose:** Actionable principle or framework the reader can use

[Write your takeaway here]

**Examples:**
- "Start with one SOP. Document it. Then automate it. Repeat."
- "Build trust in layers: manual → assisted → autonomous → YOLO. Don't skip steps."

---

### Call-to-Action (1 sentence)
**Purpose:** Invite conversation, offer resource, or suggest next step

[Write your CTA here]

**Examples:**
- "What's one workflow you'd automate if you could start small?"
- "Follow for more frameworks on building multi-agent systems."
- "DM me if you want the full pilot playbook."

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

### Content Standards
- [ ] Metric is specific and traceable (or labeled "anecdotal")
- [ ] No buzzwords ("AI magic," "revolutionary," "game-changing")
- [ ] Tone is pragmatic and builder-focused
- [ ] Example is concrete, not vague
- [ ] Takeaway is actionable

### Structure
- [ ] Hook grabs attention (metric, question, or contrarian statement)
- [ ] Insight explains "why" or shares lesson
- [ ] Example includes numbers or specific details
- [ ] Takeaway is clear and reusable
- [ ] CTA invites engagement or offers value

### Formatting
- [ ] 120-180 word count
- [ ] Maximum 2 emojis
- [ ] 4 hashtags (2 broad + 2 niche)
- [ ] Line breaks for readability
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors

- [ ] Matches AI-Whisperers tone (see BRAND_BRIEF.md)

## Notes for Writers

- **Voice:** If posting as founder (Kyrian), use first person ("I built..."). If posting as company, use third person ("We help teams...").
- **Proof points:** Always cite the 30% and 18% metrics when relevant.
- **Mistakes are OK:** Share what went wrong, not just what worked. Authenticity builds trust.
- **Link sparingly:** LinkedIn deprioritizes posts with external links. Use links in comments instead.
- **Engage after posting:** Respond to comments within 24 hours. Ask follow-up questions.
