## Company Page vs. Personal Profile Posts

### Company Page Posts Should:
- Represent the unified brand voice (not individual leaders)
- Showcase team capabilities and company updates
- Include company announcements, case studies, insights
- Feature multiple team members (collaborative)
- Drive to company website or resources
- Use company logo and brand assets

### Personal Profile Posts Should:
- Individual leader's unique voice and expertise
- Personal insights and experiences
- Build personal brand and thought leadership
- Tag the company page for cross-promotion

**Strategy:** Personal profiles do most of the posting (Phase 1-4). Company page posts 1-2x/month for major updates.

---

## Company Page Posting Strategy

### Recommended Frequency
**Phase 1-2 (Months 1-2):** 1 post/month (milestone updates)
**Phase 3+ (Months 4+):** 2 posts/month (updates + case studies)

### When to Post from Company Page:
1. **Company milestones** (funding, partnerships, team growth)
2. **Major case studies** (client success stories)
3. **Product launches** (new features, offerings)
4. **Team spotlights** (introduce leadership, culture)
5. **Industry insights** (research, whitepapers)
6. **Event announcements** (webinars, workshops)

### When to Post from Personal Profiles:
- Weekly thought leadership
- How-to guides and frameworks
- Personal experiences and lessons learned
- Daily operations and tactical advice

## Template 5: Thought Leadership / Industry Insight

**Use case:** Share frameworks, insights, research
**Frequency:** 1x/month in Phase 3+
**Tone:** Authoritative, educational, valuable

```
The Trust Levels Framework: When to let agents run autonomously 🎯

At AI-Whisperers, we get asked constantly: "How do we know when to let an AI agent make decisions without human approval?"

Here's the framework we use with every client.

**The 5 Trust Levels:**

**Level 1: Manual (Human does everything)**
Agent suggests, human decides and executes
Example: First week of ticket triage pilot
Risk: Zero automation benefit yet

**Level 2: Assisted (Agent helps, human executes)**
Agent recommends with confidence scores, human reviews and executes
Example: Triage agent suggests urgency level
Risk: Low, human has final say

**Level 3: Supervised (Agent acts, human reviews)**
Agent takes action, human reviews within [time window]
Example: Agent auto-tags tickets, human spot-checks daily
Risk: Medium, errors are caught before impact

**Level 4: Monitored (Agent acts, human audits periodically)**
Agent operates autonomously, human reviews weekly/monthly
Example: Repo health agent flags PRs, quarterly review of accuracy
Risk: Medium-high, some errors may slip through

**Level 5: YOLO Mode (Agent fully autonomous)**
Agent operates without human review, only alerts on critical issues
Example: Password reset automation
Risk: High if misapplied, but efficient for low-stakes tasks

**How to Progress Through Levels:**

Start at Level 1-2 (always).
Measure accuracy over time.
Move to next level when error rate < [threshold, e.g., 5%].
Build in rollback mechanisms at every level.

**The Mistake Most Teams Make:**

Jumping straight to Level 5 because "AI is smart now."

Trust is earned, not assumed. Even for AI.

**Our Rule:**
No agent goes to Level 4-5 until it's proven itself at Level 2-3 for at least 100 decisions.

**Download the full framework:** [Link to detailed guide]

What trust level are your AI agents running at?

#AIGovernance #MultiAgentSystems #Automation #TrustLevels #AIWhisperers
```
