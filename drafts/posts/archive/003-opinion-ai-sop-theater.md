## Post Copy (Final Version)

```
Why is your AI strategy failing? You skipped the boring part.

Most teams try to automate before they document. That's backwards. AI can't replace a process you haven't defined.

I've seen companies spend $100K on AI tools while their SOPs live in someone's head. When that person leaves, the automation breaks. The tool wasn't the problem — the foundation was missing 📋

Here's the truth: SOPs are the foundation, AI is the accelerator.

Document first. Automate second. SOPs tell the agent what "good" looks like. Without them, you're teaching AI to guess.

Our 30% handle time reduction? It started with a 2-page SOP. The AI agent didn't invent the triage process — it executed what we already knew worked.

Start small: pick one workflow, write down the steps, then automate it. If you can't explain it to a person, you can't teach it to an agent.

What's one process you run that isn't documented yet?

#AI #Automation #ProcessImprovement #SOPAutomation
```

## Potential Controversy & Mitigation

**Controversial Take:**
"AI can't replace a process you haven't defined."

**Possible Pushback:**
- "But AI can discover processes through observation!"
- "This is old-school thinking; AI is more flexible than you think."

**Mitigation:**
- Acknowledge AI's pattern recognition capabilities
- Clarify: AI can assist in documentation, but humans define intent
- Emphasize: This isn't anti-AI, it's pro-foundation

**Response Example:**
"Great point — AI *can* help discover patterns. But it still needs humans to define 'what should happen' vs. 'what currently happens.' SOPs capture intent, not just habit."
