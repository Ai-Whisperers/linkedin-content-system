## Post Copy (Final Version)

```
18% fewer hotfixes in 6 weeks. Here's what a repo-health agent actually does.

Our client had a problem: technical debt was invisible until it became a crisis. Hotfixes disrupted sprints, velocity tanked, and the team spent more time firefighting than building.

We deployed a repo-health agent that tracks code quality, test coverage, and deployment risk. It flags issues before they ship — no surprises, no late-night heroics 🛡️

After 6 weeks: 18% reduction in hotfixes, measurable improvement in code stability, and a team that trusts their CI/CD pipeline again.

The lesson: you can't fix what you can't see. The agent doesn't prevent mistakes — it makes them visible early, when they're cheap to fix.

Measure repo health like you measure uptime. Then act on it.

What's one repo metric you wish you tracked better?

#DevOps #Automation #RepoHealth #MultiAgentSystems
```
