## Content Strategy Options

### Option 1: Both Brothers Equally Visible (Recommended)
**Approach:** Both Ivan and Kyrian featured in content, both post from personal profiles

**Pros:**
- Shows collaborative leadership
- Broader network reach (2x personal networks)
- Different voices/perspectives attract diverse audiences
- Humanizes the brand (family business, trust)

**Cons:**
- Requires both brothers to commit time to LinkedIn
- Need to coordinate posting schedule to avoid overlap
- Voice consistency takes more effort

**Implementation:**
- Create separate profile copy for Ivan
- Alternate posting weeks or audiences
- Cross-reference each other's posts ("As my brother Ivan mentioned...")
- Feature both in company About section

---

### Option 2: Kyrian as Primary Voice, Ivan Behind Scenes
**Approach:** Kyrian handles public LinkedIn presence, Ivan focuses on delivery/ops

**Pros:**
- Single consistent voice (easier brand management)
- Less time commitment from Ivan
- Clear "face of the company"
- Simpler content workflow

**Cons:**
- Misses opportunity to leverage Ivan's network
- May not reflect true partnership
- Single point of failure if Kyrian unavailable

**Implementation:**
- Use current content system as-is (Kyrian-focused)
- Mention Ivan in company materials but he doesn't post
- Ivan's profile exists but minimal activity

---

### Option 3: Specialized Roles by Audience
**Approach:** Each brother targets different audiences with specialized content

**Examples:**
- Kyrian → Operations managers, hotel/corporate ops (ticket triage, SOP automation)
- Ivan → DevOps leads, engineering teams (repo health, technical debt)

**Pros:**
- Deep expertise in each domain
- Tailored messaging for different audiences
- Both brothers contribute without overlap
- Maximizes network reach with focused value

**Cons:**
- More complex content calendar (2 tracks)
- Requires clear role definition
- May fragment brand if not coordinated

**Implementation:**
- Define audience split (ops vs. engineering)
- Create 2 content tracks in calendar
- Coordinate hashtags and cross-promotion
- Both reference company page for unified brand

---

## Recommended Approach (Hybrid)

**Start with Option 2 (Kyrian primary), evolve to Option 1 or 3 based on bandwidth**

### Phase 1 (Months 1-2): Kyrian Leads
- Launch with Kyrian as primary LinkedIn voice
- Use current content system (12 posts, 3/week)
- Mention Ivan in company materials and select posts
- Ivan's profile exists, minimal activity initially

### Phase 2 (Months 3-4): Introduce Ivan
- Once rhythm is established, add Ivan to posting rotation
- Ivan posts 1x/week on engineering/DevOps topics
- Kyrian continues 2x/week on ops topics
- Cross-promote each other's content

### Phase 3 (Months 5+): Full Co-Founder Visibility
- Both brothers active, coordinated calendar
- Different audiences or alternating weeks
- Joint webinars, case studies featuring both
- "Brothers building automation" becomes part of brand story

## Final Recommendation

**Start with Kyrian as primary voice** (current system is ready), then **layer in Ivan's presence gradually** based on:
1. Kyrian's bandwidth and consistency (can he maintain 3 posts/week?)
2. Ivan's interest in LinkedIn activity
3. Audience response (are people asking about the team/founders?)

**Timeline:**
- **Months 1-2:** Kyrian solo, mention Ivan in select posts
- **Month 3:** Ivan posts 1x/month on DevOps topics (test engagement)
- **Months 4-6:** Scale to full co-founder visibility if working well

This approach de-risks the launch while keeping the door open for both brothers to be active.
