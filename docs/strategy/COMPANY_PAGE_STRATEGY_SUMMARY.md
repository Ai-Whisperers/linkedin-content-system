## 🎯 Strategic Decision: Company Page First

You've decided to make the **@AI-Whisperers company page** the primary LinkedIn presence, with personal profiles supporting through engagement and amplification.

### Why This Works Better

✅ **Sustainable:** 6 hours/week for team vs. 12+ hours managing three separate profiles
✅ **Unified Brand:** One consistent voice, easier to maintain quality
✅ **B2B Credibility:** Company page is more authoritative for business clients
✅ **Simple Analytics:** Track and optimize one account instead of three
✅ **Team Amplification:** Leaders reshare company posts = 3x reach without 3x effort
