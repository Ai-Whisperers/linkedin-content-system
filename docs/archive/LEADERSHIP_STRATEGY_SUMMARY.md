## 🔄 Cross-Promotion Guidelines

When one leader posts, others should:
1. **Like** within 1 hour of posting
2. **Comment** thoughtfully (add value, don't just say "Great!")
3. **Reshare** to networks (if highly relevant)

**Example comment from Ivan on Kyrian's post:**
> "The 30% handle time reduction came from focusing on triage only. We learned that lesson the hard way — tried to automate everything at once and nothing worked well. Starting small was the key."

This shows collaboration without being overly promotional.

## 💡 Key Insights

### Why This Strategy Works

1. **Phased Risk Reduction:** Start with Kyrian (ready now), add Ivan (Month 3), add Jonathan (Month 4)
2. **Full Funnel Coverage:** Ops → Engineering → Executive (complete buyer journey)
3. **3x Network Reach:** Three leaders = three personal networks activated
4. **Expertise Positioning:** Each leader owns their domain (no overlap/conflict)
5. **Sustainable Cadence:** 1-2 posts/week per person (manageable long-term)
6. **Brand Narrative:** Brothers + CTO = unique, relatable, credible

### Critical Success Factors

1. **Consistency:** Kyrian must maintain 3/week in Phase 1 to build momentum
2. **Quality over Quantity:** Better to post less frequently with high quality
3. **Authentic Collaboration:** Cross-promotion should add value, not be formulaic
4. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Track metrics, adjust based on performance
5. **Patient Scaling:** Don't rush to Phase 3, let each phase prove itself
