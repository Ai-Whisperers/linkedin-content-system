## Engagement Rate Calculation

**Formula:**
```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments) / Impressions × 100
```

**Example:**
- Likes: 50
- Comments: 10
- Impressions: 2,000
- Engagement Rate: (50 + 10) / 2,000 × 100 = 3%

**Benchmark:**
- **< 1%:** Low (review content relevance, hashtags, timing)
- **1-2%:** Average (solid, room for improvement)
- **2-5%:** Good (content resonates well)
- **5%+:** Excellent (high value, strong engagement)
