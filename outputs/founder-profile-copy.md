## About Section (2600 characters max)

```
I help teams build automation that actually works — not demos, but reliable tools that stick.

My approach: start small, measure early, document everything.

Recently, I helped a client cut 30% of ticket handle time with an AI triage lane and reduced hotfixes by 18% using a repo-health agent. These weren't moonshots — they were scoped pilots with clear KPIs, built in weeks, not quarters.

My brother Ivan and I co-founded AI-Whisperers to bring transparency and measurable outcomes to AI automation. We work with operations and engineering teams across Paraguay and the EU, turning standard operating procedures into multi-agent workflows that actually ship.

What I believe:
• Small, scoped agents beat big heroic promises
• SOPs are the foundation, AI is the accelerator
• Trust levels matter (manual → assisted → autonomous → YOLO)
• Every metric should be traceable or labeled "anecdotal"

What we do:
• Automation audits – Find high-ROI use cases in your workflows
• Multi-agent design – Build ticket triage, repo health, service ops agents
• SOP transformation – Turn manual processes into agent workflows
• Playbooks & governance – Sustainable systems, not one-off hacks

If you're in DevOps or operations and tired of AI hype, let's collaborate on small, useful robots that make work easier.

I share frameworks, lessons learned, and pilot results here — the real stuff that helps teams ship.
```
