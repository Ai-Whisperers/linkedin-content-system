## Content Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Running Out of Ideas
**Mitigation:**
- 5 core topics from LINKEDINCONTEXT.md provide foundation
- Repurpose content (post → carousel, carousel → blog post)
- Ask audience: "What automation challenge should I cover next?"

### Risk 2: Low Engagement Early On
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on quality over vanity metrics
- Engage proactively with target audience posts
- Share lessons learned, not just wins (builds authenticity)

### Risk 3: Founder Bandwidth
**Mitigation:**
- Batch-create posts (write 3-4 in one session)
- Use POST_TEMPLATE.md for consistency
- Schedule posts in advance (LinkedIn native scheduler)
